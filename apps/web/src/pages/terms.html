1. What CampusRide is

CampusRide is an app that connects people who need a ride around the University of Ghana, Legon, and nearby areas ("riders") with independent drivers who offer rides in their own cars ("drivers"). CampusRide does not itself provide transport. Drivers are not our employees or agents; each trip is an arrangement between the rider and the driver, and CampusRide provides the app that arranges it.

CampusRide is a new service. Features, the service area and fares may change, and the service may sometimes be unavailable. We'll try to give notice of important changes.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older. This applies to riders and drivers.
  • You need a mobile phone number that can receive text messages.
  • Drivers must also meet the requirements in section 7 and be approved by CampusRide before taking trips. We may refuse or withdraw approval at our discretion.

3. Your account

  • Use your real name and your own phone number. One account per person.
  • Keep your phone and sign-in codes to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.
  • You can delete your account at any time.
  • How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.

4. Fares and cash payment

  • The fare is shown in the app before you book. It is a fixed fare for the trip type you choose (shared or private), not a meter.
  • Shared rides: other riders going your way may join the car, up to four riders in total. Your fare never goes up because others join.
  • Payment is in cash, paid by the rider directly to the driver at the end of the trip. Please carry small change. The app does not take card or mobile money payments.
  • Drivers owe CampusRide a commission on each completed trip, shown in the Driver app, and agree to pay it as and when CampusRide asks. Unpaid commission may lead to suspension.
  • A trip a driver forgets to end is completed automatically some hours after it started, at its booked fare.
  • If you think a fare was wrong, contact support within 7 days.

5. Cancellations and no-shows

  • Riders can cancel a request or a booked trip in the app before the trip starts. There is currently no cancellation fee. If we introduce one, we will show it in the app before you book.
  • If no driver accepts your request within a short time, the app will tell you and let you keep waiting or change your ride.
  • No-shows: once the driver has arrived at your pickup, please be ready. If you don't show up within a few minutes of the driver arriving, the driver may cancel your pickup.
  • Drivers may decline requests without penalty, but should not accept a trip they don't intend to complete. Repeated cancellations after accepting may lead to suspension.

6. Riders' responsibilities

  • Set the correct pickup and drop-off points, and be at the pickup on time.
  • Check the car, number plate and driver's photo in the app match before getting in.
  • Wear a seatbelt where one is available, and respect the driver's car and other passengers.
  • Pay the fare in cash at the end of the trip.
  • You are responsible for your belongings. We'll try to help reunite lost items but can't guarantee it.

9. Safety and SOS

  • In an emergency, call 112 (Ghana's emergency number) first.
  • During a trip, a rider can press SOS in the app. It texts the rider's emergency contact (if saved) and CampusRide a private link to follow the trip. SOS depends on mobile networks and text messages, which can fail or be delayed, and it does not alert the police or an ambulance. It is not a substitute for emergency services.
  • Report any safety incident to us as soon as you can via support. We may share information with the police where the law requires or someone's safety is at risk.
