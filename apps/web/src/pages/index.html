FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I pay?

In cash, to your driver, at the end of the trip. A shared ride is {{sharedFare}} per rider and Ride alone is {{loneFare}}; the app shows the fare before you book. Please carry small change.

How do shared rides work?

Other riders going your way can join your car, up to {{maxSharedRiders}} riders in total. Each pays {{sharedFare}}, and your fare never goes up because others join. If you'd rather not share, choose Ride alone.

How do you keep riders safe?

Every driver is approved by CampusRide before they can take trips, and the app shows their name, photo, car and number plate so you can check them before you get in. During a trip, the Safety button alerts your emergency contact and CampusRide with a live link to your trip, or calls 112.

Can I cancel a ride?

Yes, in the app, any time before the trip starts, and there's no fee. If you don't come out within a few minutes of the driver arriving, the driver may cancel your pickup.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Account → Delete account. Without the app, email {{deleteEmailLink}}. The account deletion page says exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

How do I contact support?

Email {{supportEmailLink}} or call {{supportPhoneLink}}. We aim to reply within two working days. More answers are on the Support page.
