University of Ghana, Legon

Rides around campus, at a fixed fare.

CampusRide connects students and staff with approved local drivers for short trips around the University of Ghana and the areas close to it. Share a car to pay less, or take one to yourself. You see the fare before you book and pay the driver in cash.

Coming soon to Google Play Rider and driver apps for Android

How it works

  1. Pick your pickup and destination. Choose a hall, faculty, gate or landmark, or drop a pin.
  2. Choose shared or private. The fare is fixed and shown before you book.
  3. Watch your driver arrive. See the car on the map, with the driver's name, photo and plate.
  4. Pay in cash at the end. Then rate your trip.

Built for campus

1

Shared rides

Ride with up to three other people going your way and pay a lower fare each.

2

Approved drivers

Every driver's details and car are checked by CampusRide before they can take trips.

3

4

Sign in with your phone

No passwords. We text you a one-time code to sign in.

Drive with CampusRide

Have a car and know the campus? The CampusRide Driver app is coming to Google Play too. Sign up in the app, add your car and a photo, and start taking trips once you're approved. Riders pay you in cash; CampusRide's commission is shown in the app.
